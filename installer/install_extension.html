3 Quick Steps (~20 seconds)

1

Open Extensions Page

Click one of the copy buttons above (e.g. chrome://extensions), paste it into your browser address bar, and press Enter.

2

Enable Developer Mode

Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner (Chrome/Brave) or left sidebar (Edge).

3

Load Unpacked Folder

Click Load unpacked, paste the copied extension path, click Select Folder, and pin the Genshin Helper icon to your toolbar.
